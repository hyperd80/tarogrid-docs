# The example cast (global — use in every article)

One consistent fictional world, so knowledge compounds across articles. Set by owner amendment 2026-07-10 (replaces the earlier region-specific cast).

## Maya's Closet — the anchor business
An online boutique. Maya sells through her Instagram and Facebook pages; orders arrive in DMs; she ships with local couriers and many customers pay cash on delivery.

- **Products:** Rose Dress $59 · Silk Scarf $19 · Gift Bundle $69 (dress + scarf, draws down both stocks). Rose Dress stock starts at 12.
- **Customers:** Anika (repeat customer) · Tomas (new customer) · an occasional suspicious order that fails the fraud check.
- **Team:** Maya (owner) · Sana (packs parcels, books couriers) · Ben (part-time, replies to messages).

## Atlas Studio — the secondary example
A 6-person design studio run by Maya's cousin Daniel. Used for the **Work** category, team features, and client/guest access — so the docs never read as commerce-only.

## Rules
- Currency is always **$** with exact figures ($59, never "about sixty").
- No real place names, no region-specific services in examples; "your courier" unless a chapter is factually about a specific integration.
- The same numbers recur across articles (Anika's $59 order) — recognition is the point.
