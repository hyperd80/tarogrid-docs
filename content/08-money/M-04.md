*The moment you publish, an invoice stops being your editable draft and becomes a shared fact. That one-way door is the point.*

:::demo publish-invoice · "The one-way door" · 14s
Publish mints a real number — never reused, never edited after → your customer gets a public page → when they open it, you see Viewed.
:::

## What publish does

1. **Mints the number** — INV-2026-014 — from your sequence. Numbers are never reused and never skipped by accident; even two people publishing at the same instant get clean, distinct numbers.
2. **Freezes the document** — a snapshot. What your customer sees is what you saw, forever.
3. **Creates the public page** — a link anyone can open in a browser, no account. Share it in chat, email, anywhere.
4. **Starts the lifecycle** — Sent, Viewed (you'll see when they open it), Paid, Overdue ([M-05](#/M-05)). If your gateway is connected, the page carries a **Pay** button ([M-08](#/M-08)).

## Made a mistake? The honest fix

You don't edit a published invoice — you **issue a corrected document** that supersedes it, and the record shows both. Never silent history-editing.

:::why Why can't I just fix the typo?
Because your customer's copy and your copy must be the same forever — that's what makes an invoice trustworthy, to customers, accountants, and disputes. An invoice that can quietly change after sending isn't a document; it's a draft wearing a number. The immutability is the feature.
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Publish when it's ready, not to preview — the draft preview shows exactly what publishing will freeze.
- 💡 The **Viewed** badge quietly answers "did they even see it?" before you send a reminder a human would resent.

## Example

:::example
Maya publishes the boutique's $366 invoice at 9 am — INV-2026-014, link pasted into their chat. 2:14 pm: *Viewed*. Thursday: *Paid* stamps itself through the pay link, and the receipt mints. Nobody asked anybody "did you get it?"
:::
