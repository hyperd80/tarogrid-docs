**Goal:** let customers pay an invoice the moment they open it — card or mobile wallet — with the money verified before anything says Paid.

:::demo gateway-connect · "Connect once, collect forever" · 14s
Connect your payment gateway once in Settings → every public invoice grows a Pay button → verified money stamps Paid and mints the receipt.
:::

## Before you start

You need an account with a **payment gateway** — the service that actually moves card and wallet money. TaroGrid connects to *your* gateway account: their fees, your payouts, your customer's payment options.

## The steps

1. Open **Settings → Payments** and connect your gateway account (the gateway's dashboard gives you the connection details to paste).
2. **Connected ✓** — from now on, every published invoice's public page ([M-04](#/M-04)) carries a **Pay** button.
3. A customer pays → the gateway verifies the money → the invoice stamps **Paid**, the receipt mints itself, and your money picture updates ([M-01](#/M-01)).

The same connection powers [advance-payment links](#/F-24) on risky orders — one setup, two protections.

:::note
**The verification rule, everywhere:** nothing in TaroGrid says Paid because someone claims they paid. The gateway confirms real money or the status doesn't move. Screenshots are not payments — and now you never have to be the one who says so.
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ No gateway connected = no Pay button and no advance links — the buttons tell you honestly rather than pretending.
- 💡 Test with a small real invoice to yourself first — the same dress-rehearsal logic as [the shop test](#/S-10).

## Example

:::example
Maya connects her gateway on a Tuesday. Wednesday's invoice to the boutique goes out with a Pay button; Thursday it's Paid without a single "please send to this account number" message. Her advance links ([F-24](#/F-24)) started working the same moment.
:::
