## What an automation is

A sentence TaroGrid performs for you: **WHEN** something happens on a board, **DO** something specific.

- *WHEN status becomes Confirmed → book the courier.*
- *WHEN an invoice goes Overdue → send a reminder.*
- *WHEN stock hits 0 → hide the product and tell me.*

That's the whole idea. No mystery, no mood, no Friday-afternoon forgetting.

:::why Consistency, not magic
The robot does **exactly what the card says — nothing more**. It doesn't improvise, doesn't interpret, doesn't have opinions. That predictability is precisely why you can trust it with money steps: read the card, and you know everything it will ever do.
:::

## The trust rules

Worth knowing before your first switch-on (the full doctrine rides each recipe's card, [F-23](#/F-23)):

- Money-touching recipes ship **off** — you enable them knowingly.
- Rules that message a customer fire **once per order, ever** — a flapping status can't spam anyone.
- Every automated action is visible in the row's activity. Auditable or it doesn't run.
