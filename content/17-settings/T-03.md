**Goal:** bring your team in with the right amount of access on day one — and adjust it in seconds, not meetings.

:::demo role-switch · "Same workspace, different eyes" · 18s
The owner sees everything → a member without money access has no money entries at all → a guest sees one shared board, nothing else.
:::

## Inviting — link first

1. Open your **Team** (People category → Team, or ⌘K → "team").
2. **Copy an invite link** and send it in any chat — the way you already talk to your people.
3. They open it, create their account, and land in your workspace with the role you chose.
4. Change the role anytime; **remove** in one click when someone moves on — their edits and history stay (the work was the company's; the access was theirs).

## What each role feels like

The concepts live in [C-07](#/C-07); here's the practice:

:::cards
- **Owner** — everything, including money settings and access decisions. There from the start.
- **Admin** — organizes boards and people. Still needs money modules *granted* — organizing isn't auditing.
- **Member** — the day-to-day role: sees and works what they're given. Most of your team, most of the time.
- **Viewer** — reads what they're granted, changes nothing. Advisors, accountants-in-January.
- **Guest** — one shared board, nothing else exists. Clients and partners.
:::

:::tip
Start everyone at the least access that covers their whole job — widening later is one click and zero drama; narrowing later is a conversation.
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Don't hand out Admin as a compliment. It's a job description, not a rank.
- ⚠️ One human, one account — shared logins destroy the "who changed this?" answer that history exists to give ([C-06](#/C-06)).

## Example

:::example
Maya's setup, four minutes total: Sana → Member with orders access. Ben → Viewer on the Catalog. Her accountant → Viewer on Money, every January, removed in February. The wholesale supplier → Guest on the restock board. Everyone sees their whole job and nobody's browsing the net profit.
:::
