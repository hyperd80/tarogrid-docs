*Roles say who someone is. The Access Center says exactly what they can touch — module by module, person by person.*

:::demo access-center · "Pick who, pick how much" · 16s
Per module: Everyone · Owners only · Chosen people → per person: No access · View · Manage → and derived values show 🔒 Hidden — the rule that never leaks.
:::

## One window, two dials

For each money/people module — Invoices, Campaigns, Stakeholders — the Access Center offers:

:::cards
- **The module dial** — who can use it at all: **Everyone** · **Owners only** · **Chosen people**.
- **The person dial** — for the chosen: **No access** · **View** · **Manage**. Looking and touching are different grants, deliberately.
:::

## The three honest states (what people actually experience)

1. **Manage** — the full module, like the owner uses it.
2. **View** — everything visible, nothing editable. A quiet **"View only" banner** says so; the New and Edit buttons simply aren't there — absent, not grayed-out-and-taunting.
3. **No access** — the module vanishes: no sidebar entry, no search result, no locked door to rattle ([the vanish principle, C-07](#/C-07)).

## The crown rule: connection-aware privacy

Values *derived from* a module follow the module. If Sana can't access Invoices, then a customer card's "Total billed" — which is *made of* invoices — shows **🔒 Hidden** for her. Not zero (a lie), not the number (a leak). Everywhere: boards, dashboards, ASK answers.

:::why Why is Hidden better than showing nothing at all?
A permission that leaks through a side door isn't a permission. And silently *omitting* the field would make Sana's screen mysteriously different from the training video. 🔒 Hidden is the honest middle: the field exists, the value is not yours to see, and everyone understands both halves.
:::

## Checking your work: view as

Wondering what Sana actually sees? **View as** shows you her exact experience — same code, same gates, no guessing.

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Granting View "temporarily" and forgetting is the classic drift — skim the Access Center quarterly; it reads in one screen.
- 💡 "A teammate can't see X" is usually this window working as configured — check here before suspecting a bug ([C-07](#/C-07)).

## Example

:::example
Maya's configuration: Invoices → Chosen people (her + the January accountant on View). Campaigns → Owners only. Stakeholders → Everyone (the whole team serves customers). Sana's customer cards show 🔒 Hidden for totals billed — she can serve Anika perfectly without auditing her.
:::
