**Goal:** find every problem yourself, before a customer finds it for you. Ten minutes, once.

:::demo test-shop · "The dress rehearsal" · 15s

## The checklist

1. **Open your link on your phone** — a real phone, like a customer. Does the page load fast? Do photos look right? Are prices current?
2. **Place a test order** — a real product, your real address, COD.
3. **Find the row** on your Orders Board. Is every field where [the map](#/S-08) says it should be — name, phone, address, product, total?
4. **Check the ripple** — fraud light resolved? Stock counted down (if tracked)?
5. **Walk the next step you'll actually use** — confirm the order; if your courier is connected, book it and immediately cancel with the courier if needed, or stop at confirm.
6. **Clean up** — delete the test order (it goes to Trash, recoverable) and correct the stock if your test counted it down.

**Result:** you've personally witnessed every hop a real order will make. Now share the link ([S-06](#/S-06)).

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Testing on your own computer only, never a phone, misses what most customers actually see. Phone first.
- ⚠️ Don't skip step 6 — a forgotten test order becomes a mystery $59 in your money picture later.
- 💡 Re-run this checklist after big changes: new products, re-brand, new form fields. Two minutes each time.
