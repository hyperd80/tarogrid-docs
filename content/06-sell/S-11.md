**Goal:** keep your shop current — new prices, new photos, honest stock — while everything already sold stays exactly as it was sold.

:::demo edit-after-launch · "Edit live, safely" · 14s

:::

## The one rule

**Edit in the Catalog. Only in the Catalog.** It's the single source of truth ([S-02](#/S-02)) — the shop page, the product picker, and future orders all follow it automatically.

## What edits do — and don't do

- **Price change** → the live shop shows it on the next refresh. **Orders already placed keep the price they were sold at.** A price change is not a time machine — and your money records stay honest because of it.
- **Photo change** → shop updates. (An old chat preview may lag — that's the chat app's cache, [S-06](#/S-06).)
- **Stock correction** → count changes take effect immediately; the shop blocks or unblocks ordering accordingly.
- **Availability off** → the product leaves the shop but keeps its history. This is how you retire products — not deletion.

:::why Why don't old orders update when I change a price?
Because an order is a record of an agreement: Anika bought at $59 on the day it cost $59. If edits rewrote history, your revenue numbers would quietly change under your feet — and an invoice already sent would disagree with your board. Sold is sold; the Catalog is about *what's next*.
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Don't delete a product to remove it from the shop — use **availability**. Deleting orphans its history in old orders.
- ⚠️ Big sale starting? Set the **sale price** field ([S-03](#/S-03)) — don't overwrite the real price.

## Example

:::example
January. Maya raises the Rose Dress from $59 to $64, retires a discontinued scarf color with availability-off, and corrects stock after a stocktake (found one extra dress in the back). Elapsed time: three minutes. December's orders, December's invoices, December's numbers: untouched.
:::
