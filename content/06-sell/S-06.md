## Publish

In the form builder, click **Share to web**. You get your shop's address — including a **short link** made for chat: easy to paste, easy to tap, impossible to mistype.

Put it everywhere your customers already are: your bio, your pinned post, your replies, your story.

## The unfurl — your storefront's handshake

When your link is pasted into a chat app, it doesn't sit there as bare text. It **unfurls**: your shop's name, up to three real products with **live prices** (sale price shown when active), and your first product photo.

:::why Why does the preview matter so much?
Because for many shops, the chat message *is* the storefront's front window. A customer decides whether to tap in the half-second they see the preview. A real photo and a real price in that half-second beats "tap to find out" every time.
:::

## Keeping the link fresh

The live page always shows your current Catalog — edit a price and the page updates on the next refresh ([S-11](#/S-11)).

⚠️ One honest caveat: **chat apps cache previews.** If you re-brand your shop and an already-shared link still shows the old look *in the preview*, that's the chat app's memory, not your page — anyone who taps through sees the current shop. Sharing the link fresh generates a new preview.

## Pitfalls

- Don't share before [testing end-to-end](#/S-10). Two minutes; zero embarrassment.
- Don't make a new form (new link) for every promotion — links you've spread around keep working; change the *content*, keep the address.

## Example

:::example
Maya pins one message in her page's chat: *"Ordering is easy — tap here 🌹"* with her short link. The preview shows the Rose Dress at $49 (sale live), the scarf, the bundle. Customers tap, order, done — many without ever asking her a question. Her DMs got quieter and her orders went up the same week.
:::
