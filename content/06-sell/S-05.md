## The builder's sections, in order

**1 — Fields: what you ask.** TaroGrid starts you with the courier-ready core: **name, phone, address**. These aren't arbitrary — they're exactly what a courier needs to deliver a parcel, which is why booking later takes one click instead of re-typing ([Where does an order GO?](#/S-08)).

**2 — The product picker.** The heart of the form. It shows your Catalog — real photos, live prices, live stock — and lets the customer choose products, options, and quantities. You never list products by hand here; the picker *is* the Catalog, worn as a storefront.

**3 — Branding.** The form inherits your shop's look automatically ([Shop branding](#/S-07)). Any single piece — background, accent — can be overridden for *this form only*.

**4 — Share to web.** One click publishes the form as your shop link ([S-06](#/S-06)).

:::tip
Ask only what shipping needs. Every extra question costs you completed orders — a customer mid-checkout is a customer easily lost. If you're curious about something, ask it *after* they've become a customer, not before.
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Don't remove the phone field to "reduce friction." Phone is the identity key — it's how orders, customers, and fraud checks all connect ([S-08](#/S-08)).
- Don't build a second form for a second product — the picker carries your whole Catalog. Multiple forms are for genuinely different *audiences*, not different products.
