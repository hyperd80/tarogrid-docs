## What the customer experiences

1. **They tap your link** — it opens in their browser. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.
2. **They see your shop**: your brand, your products with photos and live prices, sale prices honestly struck through.
3. **They pick products** — options and quantities included; out-of-stock items can't be picked.
4. **They fill the courier-ready core**: name, phone, address.
5. **They choose how to pay** — the options *you* enabled.
6. **They tap Order** — and get a clear confirmation. On your side, the row lands ([S-08](#/S-08)).

## The payment options

:::cards
- **💵 Cash on delivery (COD)** — they pay the courier at the door. The amount rides with the courier booking and is tracked in *Courier Cash* until it's truly yours.
- **💳 Pay online** — through your connected payment gateway; the order arrives already paid.
- **🛡️ Advance payment** — a small upfront amount (about the delivery fee) on risky orders — the anti-fake-order shield ([F-24](#/F-24)).
:::

:::note
COD is beloved by customers and risky for sellers — that's not a contradiction TaroGrid ignores; it's the one it's built for. The [Fraud Check](#/F-21) and [advance requests](#/F-24) exist precisely so you can offer COD *and* sleep.
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Online payments need your payment gateway connected first (*Get paid online*, in the Money chapter) — until then, the option simply doesn't show.
- Don't promise delivery timing on the form that your courier can't keep — the form is a promise; couriers are the weather.

Tomas, a first-time customer, opens Maya's link at midnight. Picks a Gift Bundle, chooses COD, types his address, taps Order — ninety seconds, no account. On Maya's board: a new row, fraud light 🟡 (new number, thin history). The [risky-order play](#/F-21) takes it from here.
