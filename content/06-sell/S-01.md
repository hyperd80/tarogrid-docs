*Everything about selling in TaroGrid is one road with three stops. This chapter walks it stop by stop; this article shows you the whole road first.*

:::demo golden-path · "The Golden Path" · 25s
Catalog (photo, price, stock) → shop link (a public page, no app) → Orders Board (one row per order) → fraud checked, stock counted, courier ready.
:::

:::figure golden-path
:::

## The three stops

**Stop 1 — the Catalog.** Your shelf. Every product lives here once: photo, price, sale price, stock, options, bundles. One truth — everything else reads from it.

**Stop 2 — the Sales Form, published as your shop link.** A public page any customer can open in a browser. No app to install, no account to create. It shows your products and asks only what delivery needs.

**Stop 3 — the Orders Board.** Every completed form becomes one row: customer, phone, address, products, total, status *Pending*. You never type an order that came through the link.

## What happens after the third stop

The moment a row lands, TaroGrid keeps working:

- The **fraud check** runs — a risk light before you ship (*[Fraud Check field](#/F-21)*).
- **Stock counts down** — including every member of a bundle (*[Product Catalog field](#/F-19)*).
- The **money side** learns an order worth $59 exists.
- **Automations** can take over — confirm → book courier → track → collect (*the Orders & Delivery chapter, coming*).

:::why Why a road instead of a pile of features?
Because selling *is* a sequence: shelf → storefront → order → delivery → cash. When the tools mirror the sequence, each step hands honestly to the next — the price on the shop is the catalog's price, the row's total is the real total, the courier gets the address the customer actually typed. No re-typing anywhere on the road means no place for errors to sneak in.
:::

## Example

:::example
**Maya's road, end to end.** Maya puts the Rose Dress ($59, stock 12) on her shelf → publishes her link → pastes it under her latest post. At 9:12 pm Anika taps it, fills three fields, taps **Order**. At 9:13 Maya has a row: 🟢, stock 11, total $59. Maya's part so far: zero typing.
:::
