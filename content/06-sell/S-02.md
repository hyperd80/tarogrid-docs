## Overview

The **Product Catalog** is a Module Window ([Idea 3](#/C-03)) — the control center for everything you sell. Under it lives a real board with one row per product. Everything about a product lives here:

- **Photo(s)** — what the shop shows.
- **Price**, and an optional **sale price** that wins automatically while it's set.
- **Stock** — tracked (counts down on every sale) or untracked (for services and made-to-order).
- **Options** — size, color ([Variants](#/S-03)).
- **Bundles** — combos with their own price ([Combos](#/S-04)).
- **Availability** — take something off the shop without deleting it.

## Why a Catalog instead of typing product names?

:::why One truth for price and stock
A typed product name can't count stock, can't price itself, can't power a shop page. A real product object can. With one Catalog, your shop, your orders, your invoices, and your profit math can never disagree — they're all reading the same card.
:::

## How to add your first product

1. Press **⌘K**, type "cat", open the **Product Catalog**.
2. Add the product: clear photo, real name, real price.
3. Tracking stock? Enter the count. Not tracking? Leave it — nothing will count down.

**You should see:** the product as a card, ready for the shop.

## Example

:::example
**Maya's shelf.** Three cards: Rose Dress $59 (stock 12) · Silk Scarf $19 (stock 30) · Gift Bundle $69 (the dress and scarf together — [Combos](#/S-04)). When the holiday rush starts, she sets the dress's sale price to $49 — and her shop, her order totals, and her invoices all show $49 the same instant, because they never had separate copies to begin with.
:::

## What to avoid

- ⚠️ Don't "fix" a price by editing an order. Fix the Catalog — the one true place. (Genuine one-off discounts have their own honest override, per order, that never touches the Catalog.)
- Don't create duplicate products for options — one product, with variants ([S-03](#/S-03)).

## Best practices

- ✔ Real photos, taken once, reused everywhere.
- ✔ Set stock honestly — the shop stops overselling for you.
- ✔ Retire products with **availability**, not deletion — history stays intact.
