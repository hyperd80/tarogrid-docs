*Symptom first, then the fix — in the order these actually come up.*

## "My customer says the link doesn't open."

Most often it's their in-app browser struggling. Ask them to open the link in their normal browser (Chrome, Safari). If it opens for you and not for them, it's their connection — the page itself is served from the cloud and doesn't depend on your device being online.

## "The preview in chat shows the old price / old look."

Chat apps cache link previews ([S-06](#/S-06)). Anyone who *taps through* sees the current shop. Sharing the link in a fresh message generates a new preview.

## "A product isn't showing on the shop."

Three checks, in order: is its **availability** on? Is its **stock** above zero (tracked stock at zero hides ordering)? Did you **publish** after creating it? One of these three, almost every time.

## "My customer ordered but I don't see it."

First: breathe — then check the top of the Orders Board for a *Pending* row, and your board's filters (a filtered view can hide new rows). The full checklist lives in *"My order didn't appear"* in the Fix-it chapter (coming). The order journey itself: [Where does an order GO?](#/S-08).

## "Can customers order without a phone number?"

No — and that's protection, not stubbornness. Phone is the identity key ([S-08](#/S-08)): it's how the courier reaches them, how fraud checks work, and how repeat customers match instead of duplicating.

## "Someone placed an obviously fake order."

That's what the fraud light is for ([F-21](#/F-21)) — and the [advance-payment shield](#/F-24) converts real buyers and filters fake ones without you playing detective.

## "Can I pause the shop?"

Yes — unpublish the form, or set products unavailable. The link then shows an honest "not taking orders right now" rather than an error. Republishing brings the same link back to life.
