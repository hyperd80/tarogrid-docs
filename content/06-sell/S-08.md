## Every answer, one home

The Sales Form and the Orders Board are two faces of the same thing. Each form field writes exactly one board column:

:::figure form-mapping
:::

Two of these deserve a closer look:

**Phone is the identity key.** It's how TaroGrid recognizes people. When Anika orders again next month, her phone number matches her existing customer record — she's *matched*, not duplicated. One human, one row in People, her whole history attached.

**Products are real catalog references** — not typed names. That's why the total prices itself from the Catalog (sale price winning automatically), and why stock can count down the instant the order lands ([Product Catalog field](#/F-19)).

## What TaroGrid adds by itself

Beyond your customer's answers, every order row arrives with:

:::cards
- **🕒 Arrival time** — when the order came in, to the minute.
- **🟢 Fraud check** — the risk light, already resolved before you open the row ([F-21](#/F-21)).
- **Status — at your workflow's start** — new orders arrive at your board's first stage (*Pending*, in Maya's setup): the gate your automations watch.
:::

## The ripple

The row landing is the *start*, not the end:

1. **Stock counts down** — every member of a bundle included.
2. **The customer is created-or-matched by phone** — no duplicate Anikas, ever.
3. **The money side sees the order** — totals, outstanding, eventually the invoice.
4. **Automations wake up** — confirm-to-book-courier and friends, if you've turned them on.

## The honesty note

:::note
The form is just a friendly door into the Orders Board. Everything it writes, you could write by hand — the form is simply faster and mistake-proof. There is no hidden second system: what you see on the board *is* the order, all of it.
:::
