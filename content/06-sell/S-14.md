## What's coming

Today your shop lives on a TaroGrid link ([S-06](#/S-06)). The private-domain wizard will let it live on **your own web address** — like `shop.yourbrand.com` — while everything behind it (Catalog, orders, the whole Golden Path) works exactly the same.

## What connecting a domain means, in plain English

A domain is the address you bought (yourbrand.com). Connecting it to your shop means adding **one small setting** at the company where you bought it — a note that says "shop.yourbrand.com points to my TaroGrid shop." The wizard will show you the exact setting to copy and confirm, in green, when it's working. No technical background needed; it's a two-minute copy-paste with guidance.

## Why it will be worth it

- **Trust** — customers see *your* name in the address bar.
- **Portability** — links you print on packaging and cards are yours forever.
- **One brand everywhere** — pairs with your sending domain for email, when you set up campaigns.

:::note
This will be a paid-plan feature when it ships. Your existing TaroGrid short link keeps working forever either way — a custom domain adds an address, it doesn't take one away.
:::

## Until then

Your short link is fully yours to spread — bio, pinned posts, packaging. When the wizard ships, the same shop simply gains a second, branded front door.
