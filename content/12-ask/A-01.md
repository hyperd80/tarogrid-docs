*One bar, three superpowers. Most people use the first for a week, then never stop using all three.*

:::demo ask-three · "Find · Ask · Act" · 16s
FIND: three letters, any window or board → ASK: a question answered from your data → ACT: a sentence becomes work, after you confirm.
:::

## The three superpowers

:::cards
- **FIND** — type a few letters, jump anywhere: windows, boards, people, settings, even help articles. The end of menu-hunting ([W-04](#/W-04)).
- **ASK** — question your own data: *"revenue this month" · "how many pending orders"*. Answers come as numbers and charts, from your boards, instantly.
- **ACT** — describe work and it happens: *"spent 20 on boost"* ([M-09](#/M-09)) · *"create an order for Anika — 2 × Silk Scarf"* ([the cascade, A-04](#/A-04)).
:::

## The promise: instant, private, predictable

ASK is **deterministic** — it understands set phrasings, the same way every time. Not an AI guessing at you: no waiting, no cloud round-trip reading your business, no surprise interpretations, no per-question bill. Type the phrase, get the behavior, always.

## The safety contract

Acting has rules, and they never bend:

- **Understood-chips first.** ASK shows what it understood — *Ads · $20 · today* — as chips you can correct before anything happens.
- **Confirm cards always.** No write happens from typing alone; you always see and approve the card.
- **Undo, as one step.** Even a multi-part cascade reverses with one undo ([A-04](#/A-04)).

:::why Why show me what you understood?
Because a bar that acts on a guess is a bar you'll stop trusting the first time it guesses wrong. The chips make ASK's understanding *visible* before it becomes real — you approve meaning, not hope.
:::

## Example

:::example
Maya's fastest minute: **⌘K** *"cour"* → Courier Cash (find). **⌘K** *"revenue this month"* → $1,240 (ask). **⌘K** *"spent 20 on boost"* → confirm card → logged (act). Three superpowers, no menus, fourteen seconds.
:::
