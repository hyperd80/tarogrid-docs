*For orders that arrive by phone call or chat — the ones your shop link never sees — one sentence does what four screens used to.*

:::demo ask-order · "A sentence becomes a business event" · 22s
Type “create an order for Anika — 2 × Silk Scarf” → one card previews the whole cascade → everything editable before confirm → confirm: it all happens; undo: it all reverses, as one step.
:::

## The cascade, on one card

Type the sentence and ASK shows **one card** previewing everything it's about to create: the **customer** (matched or new) → the **order** → a **draft invoice** → the receipt arrangement. What it understood appears as chips; nothing exists until you confirm.

## Everything on the card is editable

- **Products** — a picker per line, real catalog items, quantity steppers.
- **Prices** — from the Catalog (sale-aware) unless you override a line: a genuine one-off override that never touches the Catalog ([F-19](#/F-19)).
- **The customer** — matching is *visible*: **"✓ Using existing customer: Anika"** with an explicit **"Not them — create new"** escape. A near-match is never silently merged.

## The honesty rules the card lives by

:::cards
- **Matched says matched** — never "created." You always know which happened.
- **Matching fills only empty fields** — an existing phone number is never overwritten by a guess.
- **A new customer needs a phone or valid email** — the identity rule, everywhere ([S-08](#/S-08)).
- **No invoice access? The invoice step is skipped — and the card says so.** The order still proceeds ([who sees money, C-07](#/C-07)).
- **The receipt line reads "automatic on payment"** — receipts mint themselves when money arrives ([M-01](#/M-01)); the card won't pretend otherwise.
:::

## Confirm — and the one-step undo

Confirm, and the cascade happens: customer, order, draft invoice, all real, all visible. Changed your mind? **Undo reverses the entire cascade as one step** — no hunting down three objects across three windows ([the safety net, B-11](#/B-11)).

## Example

:::example
Anika messages Maya directly: "two scarves please 🧣". Maya: **⌘K**, *"create an order for Anika — 2 × Silk Scarf"*. The card: Anika ✓ matched · 🧣 ×2 · $38 · draft invoice ready. She bumps it to 3 scarves on the card (Anika added one), confirms. Four things exist. Time: twenty seconds, phone still in her other hand.
:::
