## Walk the row, left to right

**The product chip** ([F-19](#/F-19)) is a real catalog reference — which is why the next cell over, **the total**, priced itself correctly, sale price and all, with nobody typing a number.

**The fraud light** ([F-21](#/F-21)) resolved the moment the row landed — reading the phone number the Sales Form deliberately collected ([S-05](#/S-05)).

**The courier cell** ([F-20](#/F-20)) can book with one click *because* the form collected exactly what a courier needs — and it carries the COD amount from the total automatically.

**The COD marker** means the delivered cash gets tracked in Courier Cash until the payout truly arrives.

## The point of the anatomy

:::why No field is alone
Each selling field is useful by itself. Together they form a chain where every link feeds the next: catalog → true price → known customer → risk light → one-click courier → tracked cash. Break any link — type products by hand, skip the phone field, book couriers in a portal — and the chain downstream of the break goes manual. Keep the chain, and an order runs itself from tap to cash.
:::

## The chain, with the automations on

With the recipes from [the cookbook](#/F-23) turned on, the full sequence reads like this: order lands (🟢) → *you confirm* → courier books itself → delivered → COD receivable appears → payout reconciles. **Your one human decision per order: confirm.** Everything else is the chain.

For risky orders, the chain has a guard: 🔴 → advance requested → paid → *then* it joins the normal flow ([F-24](#/F-24)).
