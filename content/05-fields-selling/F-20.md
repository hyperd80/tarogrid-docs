*The second selling superpower: the courier lives in the row — not in a separate portal your board can't see.*

:::demo courier-book · "Confirm → booked → delivered → cash" · 24s
Confirmed → the courier books itself from the row → the tracking ID types itself in → status updates alone: in transit, delivered → the cash appears in Courier Cash.
:::

## What is it?

A field that connects an order row to a real courier service and manages the shipment *from the cell*: book the parcel, receive the tracking ID, and watch the delivery status update itself.

## Why it exists

:::why The stale-board problem
Copy-pasting addresses into a courier portal is slow and typo-prone. Worse: once booked *there*, your board *here* stops knowing the truth — is it shipped? delivered? returned? This field keeps one truth: the courier's reality, visible on your row, current by itself.
:::

## How it works

1. **Connect a courier once** — your courier account details go into Settings, one time.
2. On any order row, the cell offers **Book**. TaroGrid sends the delivery details — name, phone, address, amount to collect — **auto-filled from the row** (which the Sales Form collected courier-ready in the first place, [S-05](#/S-05)).
3. The **tracking ID writes itself back** into the cell.
4. A **status watcher keeps Delivery Status current**: booked → in transit → delivered (or returned), without you checking any portal.
5. On *Delivered*, a cash-on-delivery amount flows into **Courier Cash** — tracked until the courier actually pays you (*the Orders & Delivery chapter, coming*).

## The recipes

- ***Status → Confirmed ⇒ book courier*** — the dispatch recipe; confirming an order ships it.
- *Delivery Status → Delivered ⇒ mark the COD receivable.*
- *Returned ⇒ notify me + prompt a restock.*

All in [the cookbook](#/F-23).

## Example

:::example
Sana's morning: open the board, scan the fraud lights, select the safe *Pending* rows, mark them **Confirmed**. The dispatch recipe books every parcel; tracking IDs fill themselves in while she packs. She never opened a courier portal, and never re-typed an address — which is why no parcel has gone to a mistyped street since.
:::

## What to avoid

- ⚠️ Don't edit the tracking ID by hand — it's the courier's identifier, and the status watcher depends on it being real.
- ⚠️ Don't book the same row twice. TaroGrid guards against it — the guard is a protection, not an obstacle: one row, one parcel.

## Best practices

- ✔ Confirm is the trigger — make "Confirmed" mean *checked and ready to ship*, and let the recipe do the rest.
- ✔ Watch for *Returned* — pair it with the restock prompt so returned goods re-enter your stock instead of a drawer.
