*The first of the selling superpowers: a field that holds real products instead of words about products.*

:::demo product-field · "A product lives in the cell" · 18s
A real product sits in the cell as a chip → tap it: photo, price, stock, live from the Catalog → orders draw stock down, bundles draw down every member.
:::

## What is it?

A field that holds real products *from your Catalog* — with photo, price, and stock — instead of a typed name. The cell shows a **product chip**; tap it to peek at the photo, price, stock, and jump to the product in the Catalog.

*Analogy:* a typed name is a note that says "one red dress." A product chip is the dress's barcode — scan it and everything about the dress is right there, current.

## Why it exists

:::why Typed names can't do the job
A typed product name can't count stock, can't price itself, can't power a shop page, can't notice a sale. Real product references can — and that difference is most of what makes TaroGrid a *selling* tool instead of a list tool.
:::

## How it works

- The cell **references the Catalog** — the single source of truth ([S-02](#/S-02)). Rename the product there; every chip follows. No orphaned old names.
- Line items carry a **quantity**; the **price comes from the Catalog**, sale price winning automatically.
- **Stock draws down when an order is placed** — and a bundle draws down *each member's* stock ([Combos](#/S-04)).

## Where you'll use it

Everywhere selling happens: the Orders Board (where [the Sales Form writes it for you](#/S-08)), quotes and invoices, and any board of your own where "which product?" is a fact worth keeping.

## The Sales Form relationship

When a customer orders on your public form, TaroGrid writes this field for you — the order row's products **are** catalog references. That's why stock, pricing, and the money side stay truthful without you typing anything.

## Recipes that watch this field

- *Stock hits 0 → set product unavailable & notify me.*
- *Order placed → draw down stock* — built-in behavior, listed so you know it's the field working, not luck.

The full cookbook: [Selling-field recipes](#/F-23).

## Example

:::example
Maya's board has a row: *Anika · 🌹 Rose Dress ×1 · $49*. That $49 wasn't typed — the sale was live, the chip knew. When Maya later ends the sale, this order still says $49 (sold is sold, [S-11](#/S-11)) — but the *chip* still points at the real dress, stock, history and all.
:::

## What to avoid

- ⚠️ Don't edit a price inside an order to "fix" the catalog — fix the **Catalog**. (Genuine one-off discounts have an honest per-order override that never touches the Catalog.)
- Don't keep a separate "product name" text column beside the chip "just in case" — that's the duplicate-truth trap this field exists to close.

## Best practices

- ✔ Let the form write this field; type it yourself only for phone-in orders.
- ✔ One chip per product per line — quantities belong in the quantity, not in the name.
