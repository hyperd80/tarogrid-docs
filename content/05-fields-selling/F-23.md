*Reference page. Each recipe is one sentence of plain English that TaroGrid performs exactly, every time. Money-touching recipes ship OFF by default — you turn them on knowingly.*

## The dispatch chain

:::cards
- **Confirm ⇒ book courier** — *When an order's status becomes Confirmed, book the courier from the row.* The workhorse ([F-20](#/F-20)). Default: off until your courier is connected.
- **Delivered ⇒ COD receivable** — *When delivery status becomes Delivered, record the cash-on-delivery amount as money the courier owes you.* Feeds Courier Cash. Default: on with COD.
- **Returned ⇒ notify + restock prompt** — *When a delivery comes back, tell me and offer to put the stock back.* Restock is a prompt, not automatic — you confirm what actually returned intact. Default: on.
:::

## The risk shield

:::cards
- **Fraud 🔴 ⇒ request advance** — *When an order lands red, send the customer a small advance-payment link (about the delivery fee, 24-hour expiry).* The headline pairing of [F-21](#/F-21) + [F-24](#/F-24). Default: **off** — deliberately.
- **Advance paid ⇒ confirm order** — *When the advance is verified paid, move the order to Confirmed* — where the dispatch chain takes over. Default: on when advances are on.
- **Advance expired ⇒ flag for review** — *If the link expires unpaid, flag the order for a human look.* Never auto-cancels — the default is deliberately kind. Default: on when advances are on.
:::

## The shelf-keepers

:::cards
- **Stock 0 ⇒ unavailable + notify** — *When a product's stock hits zero, hide it from the shop and tell me.* No overselling, no surprises. Default: on for tracked stock.
- **Order placed ⇒ draw down stock** — *Every order counts its products down — bundles count every member.* Built-in behavior of [F-19](#/F-19), listed so you know it's by design.
:::

## Reading a recipe before you switch it on

Every recipe card in the app answers the same four questions: **what it watches · what it does · what it writes · its default.** If any answer surprises you, don't enable it — read its deep article first (each card links one).

:::note
Rules that message a customer fire **once per order, ever** — a status flickering back and forth can't spam anyone. And money-touching actions are visible in the row's activity, always. Automation you can't audit is automation you can't trust.
:::
