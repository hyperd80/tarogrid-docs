## How it works

In [Courier Cash](#/S-19), couriers whose connection supports statements show a **Fetch payouts** button. One click:

1. TaroGrid pulls the courier's payout statement through your connected account ([S-17](#/S-17)).
2. Each line is matched to your orders — tracking ID first, order ID second.
3. You get the honest summary: *"6 orders reconciled · 1 line needs review."* Matched orders flip to **Reconciled**; the odd one waits for your eyes.

## Availability — said plainly

🧪 Not every courier's connection can do this — statement access depends on what each courier's system offers, and the list grows. The **Connected ✓** note in Settings tells you per courier ([S-17](#/S-17)). Where fetching isn't available, [manual entry](#/S-21) does the same job with sixty more seconds of typing — the matching and the honesty are identical.

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Fetching doesn't invent matches — a line the matcher can't place goes to Needs review, same as manual. The button saves typing, not judgment.
- 💡 Fetch on the courier's payout day; a statement fetched mid-cycle is honestly incomplete, not broken.

## Example

Payout day. Maya clicks Fetch payouts: seven lines arrive, six reconcile instantly, one needs review — a return fee deduction she confirms against the returned parcel from last week ([S-23](#/S-23)). Total ritual: ninety seconds, and the money math is closed.
