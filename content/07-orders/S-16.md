**Goal:** turn the morning pile of Pending orders into booked parcels — in minutes, not an hour of portal-typing.

## The daily loop

1. Open the Orders Board — new orders sit in *Pending*, fraud lights resolved.
2. **Scan the lights.** Greens are ready. Yellows get a quick confirmation message. Reds go to the [risky-order play](#/S-22).
3. **Select the ready rows and confirm them together** — one status change for the lot.
4. If the **dispatch recipe** is on (*Confirmed ⇒ book courier*, [F-23](#/F-23)), every confirmed order books itself: details sent, tracking IDs written back while you pack.

No recipe yet? Each row's courier cell offers **Book** — one click each ([F-20](#/F-20)).

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Don't confirm before checking stock reality — Confirmed is a promise, and the recipe ships promises immediately.
- ⚠️ Confirm means *ship it*. If your team uses it to mean "seen it," the dispatch recipe will ship things you only meant to acknowledge. Pick a meaning; the robot believes you.
- 💡 Bulk-confirm greens only. Yellows and reds deserve their extra minute.

## Example

:::example
Sana's 10 am: fourteen Pending orders. Eleven green — selected, confirmed, booked by the recipe before she's taped the third box. Two yellow — quick messages sent, confirmed after replies. One red — advance link, [see the play](#/S-22). Total desk time: eight minutes.
