**Goal:** the one-time setup that turns "copy the address into the courier's website" into a single click on the order row.

:::demo courier-connect · "Once, then never again" · 10s
Your courier account details go into Settings, once → connected: every order row can now book with one click.
:::

## Before you start

You need a **merchant account with your courier** — the same one you'd use on their own website. TaroGrid connects to *your* account: their fees, their pickups, their payouts, all still yours.

## The steps

1. Open **Settings → Couriers** (or press **⌘K** and type "courier").
2. Pick your courier from the available connections — the list depends on which services TaroGrid supports in your region, and it grows.
3. Paste your merchant account details from the courier's merchant panel.
4. TaroGrid verifies them and shows **Connected ✓** — with a note on what this courier's connection supports (booking, live tracking, payout statements).

**Result:** every order row's courier cell now offers **Book** ([F-20](#/F-20)) — and the dispatch recipe can ship your mornings for you ([S-16](#/S-16)).

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Details go into Settings once — not into each order. Entering them per-order means something is wrong; stop and check Settings.
- ⚠️ Connections differ: some couriers support fetching payout statements ([S-20](#/S-20)), some only booking and tracking. The Connected note tells you honestly what you have.
- 💡 Multiple couriers? Connect them all — each order row lets you choose which one carries it.
