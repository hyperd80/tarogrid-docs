*Every order in TaroGrid walks the same road. Learn the stops once, and any board full of orders reads like a story.*

:::demo order-life · "Six stops" · 20s
Born Pending → you confirm (your one decision) → booked, in transit, delivered — by itself → reconciled: the courier's payout matched your row.
:::

## The stops

:::cards
- **Pending** — just arrived from your shop link ([S-08](#/S-08)), fraud light already on. Nothing has been promised yet.
- **Confirmed** — you checked it and said *ship it*. This is the trigger automations wait for.
- **Booked** — the courier has the parcel's details and a tracking ID exists ([F-20](#/F-20)).
- **In transit** — moving. The status updates itself; no portal-checking.
- **Delivered** — the customer has it. For COD, the courier now holds your money — which is a stop, not the end.
- **Reconciled** — the courier's payout matched this order ([S-19](#/S-19)). *Now* it's over.
:::

## The one human decision

Between Pending and Confirmed sits the only step that needs judgment: *is this order real, and can I fulfill it?* The fraud light answers the first half ([F-21](#/F-21)); your stock answers the second. Everything after Confirmed can run on rules ([S-16](#/S-16)).

:::why Why does Delivered ≠ done?
Because with cash on delivery, "delivered" means *the courier has your money*, not you. Sellers who stop tracking at Delivered are trusting a wallet they can't see. The Reconciled stop exists so the story only ends when the cash is verifiably yours.
:::

## Example

:::example
Anika's order: Pending at 9:12 pm Tuesday → Sana confirms 10:03 am Wednesday → booked itself within a minute → in transit Thursday → delivered Friday → reconciled the next Wednesday when the courier's payout arrived and matched. Six stops, one click of Sana's.
:::
