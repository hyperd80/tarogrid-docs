**Goal:** the courier paid you — record it once, and watch the right orders flip to Reconciled.

:::demo manual-remit · "Log it once" · 10s
Log what the courier paid → matched orders flip to Reconciled together.
:::

## The steps

1. In [Courier Cash](#/S-19), choose the courier and **Log payout**.
2. Enter the payout: date, total, and the lines — which parcels, which amounts (the courier's payout message or statement has these; tracking IDs are ideal).
3. TaroGrid matches lines to orders — tracking ID first, order ID second — and flips the matched ones to **Reconciled**.
4. Anything that doesn't match cleanly goes to **Needs review**, exactly like automatic fetching. Same honesty, manual keys.

:::tip
The fastest habit: log the payout the day it arrives, while the courier's message is still at the top of your chat. A month of unlogged payouts is an afternoon of archaeology; one payout is a minute.
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Don't log a lump sum with no lines "to save time" — matching is per order, and per order is where missing money hides ([the why, S-19](#/S-19)).
- ⚠️ Deductions (return fees, COD charges) belong in the payout as the courier lists them — the mismatch you'd otherwise see *is* those deductions.

## Example

:::example
Maya's courier pays weekly by bank transfer with a summary message: five parcels, $285 after fees. She logs the five lines; five orders flip Reconciled; the fee deduction matches the courier's rate card. Four minutes, and "did I get everything?" has an answer instead of a feeling.
:::
