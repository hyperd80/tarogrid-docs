You're about to trust TaroGrid with your orders and your money records. You deserve a plain answer to two questions: *where does my data live?* and *what happens when the internet dies?*

2. An edit on the desktop flows up to the cloud, then down to the phone.
3. The phone goes into airplane mode ✈. Edits keep working — they queue up with a small counter.
4. The connection returns. The queue drains, in order. All copies match again.

## The short answer

Your data lives in **two places at once**:

- **On your device.** TaroGrid keeps a full copy of your workspace right on your computer or phone. This is why the app opens fast and works offline.
- **In the cloud.** TaroGrid's server keeps the shared copy — the one your other devices and your teammates read from.

Every change you make is saved to your device *immediately*, then sent to the cloud. The cloud passes it to your other devices and your team.

## What happens when the internet is off

You keep working. Add orders, edit rows, write notes — everything saves to your device's copy. Your changes wait in a queue.

The moment your connection returns, the queue uploads **in order**, and everything catches up. You don't have to do anything, and nothing is lost in between.

:::why Why keep a copy on my device at all?
Because your shop shouldn't stop when the internet does. Load-shedding, a bad connection at the market, a rickshaw ride — none of it should lock you out of your own orders. Local-first means the app is *yours* first, connected second.
:::

## What if two people edit the same thing?

If you and Shanta edit *different* things — even different columns of the same row — both changes simply apply. That's the normal case.

If you both edit the *same cell* while apart, TaroGrid doesn't silently pick a winner. It shows you both versions and asks you to choose — and the version you don't pick stays in the cell's history, so nothing is destroyed.

## Your safety net, in layers

- **Undo** reverses your last edits — even a 500-row import reverses as one step.
- **Trash** holds deleted items so a delete is never instantly final.
- **History** — every cell quietly remembers what it said before, who changed it, and when.
- Truly irreversible actions always show an honest confirmation that says so, plainly, before anything happens.

💬 **Note** — you can export your boards whenever you want. Your data is yours; TaroGrid never holds it hostage.

## One warning that matters

⚠️ **On a shared or public computer, sign out when you finish.** Signing out on the web clears TaroGrid's local copy from that machine. If you only close the tab, your company's data stays on a computer other people use.

## Example

🛍️ **Meem, during load-shedding.** The power dies at 8 pm, and her wifi with it. Orders from her shop link still land safely in the cloud. On her phone (mobile data off, battery saving), she keeps packing and updates six order statuses — all queued. At 9:14 the current returns, her laptop wakes, the queue drains, and both devices agree again. Nobody lost anything, including her patience.
