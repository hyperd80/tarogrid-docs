**Goal:** three small victories, each under ten minutes. At the end, you'll have *seen* an order flow through TaroGrid with your own eyes — and that sight teaches more than any article.

:::demo first-three-wins · "The golden path, compressed" · 40s
A product card gets a photo and price → the shop link publishes and copies → a test order goes in on a phone → the row appears by itself, stock counts down.
:::

## Win 1 — Put one real product on your shelf

1. Open the **Product Catalog** (press **⌘K**, type "cat", Enter).
2. Add a product: a clear photo, the real name, the real price.
3. If you track stock, enter how many you have. If not, leave stock untracked — that's fine.

**You should see:** your product as a card, photo and price on it. This card is the single source of truth — your shop, your orders, and your invoices will all read price and stock from *here*.

## Win 2 — Publish your shop link

1. Open the **Sales Form** builder.
2. TaroGrid starts you with the fields a courier needs: name, phone, address. Add the product picker if it isn't there.
3. Click **Share to web** and copy your short link.

**You should see:** a link short enough to paste in any chat. That link *is* your shop — customers open it in a browser, no app, no account.

:::tip
Ask only for what shipping needs. Every extra question on the form costs you completed orders.
:::

## Win 3 — Order from yourself

1. Open your shop link on your own phone, like a customer would.
2. Place an order — one product, your real address.
3. Now watch the app: a new row appears on your **Orders Board**. The fraud check resolves. If you track stock, it just counted down.

**You should see:** an order you never typed, sitting in your board as a complete row — customer, phone, address, product, total, status *Pending*.

⚠️ When you're done admiring it, delete the test order. It goes to Trash, so nothing is ever truly lost by one click.

## What just happened (the part worth understanding)

:::example
That row didn't come from typing — it came from the form writing directly into your Orders Board. Customer answers became columns. The product became a real catalog reference, which is why stock moved. This is the whole trick of TaroGrid: **the form is a friendly door into your database.** Everything else — couriers, invoices, courier cash — builds on rows that arrive this way.
:::
