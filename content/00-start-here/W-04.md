Every screen in TaroGrid is built from the same five areas. Learn them once; nothing will ever feel unfamiliar again.

2. ① the Sidebar glows, then ② the board area, then ③ a module window floats in.
3. ④ the ASK bar opens with ⌘K, and ⑤ the help button pulses.

## The five areas

| # | Area | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| ① | **Sidebar** | Your company's map: the Money, People, and Work categories, and every board. If it exists, it's reachable from here. |
| ② | **Board area** | Where a board's rows and columns show. This is your data, in the open. |
| ③ | **Module Windows** | Smart screens that float over the board area — Invoice Center, Courier Cash, the Command Center. Close one and you're back where you were. |
| ④ | **ASK bar** | Press **⌘K** anywhere. Type to find any window or board, ask a question ("revenue this month"), or do work ("create an order for Anika"). |
| ⑤ | **Help** | The `?` opens help for the screen you're on — including this article. |

## "I want to… → where do I look?"

| You want to… | Look at |
|---|---|
| See or edit orders, products, any list | ② the board |
| Make an invoice, check courier cash, see your business at a glance | ③ a module window (open it from ① or ④) |
| Find *anything*, fast | ④ ASK — type a few letters |
| Add a teammate, change settings | ① sidebar, bottom section |
| Understand the screen you're on | ⑤ the `?` |

💡 **Tip** — the fastest habit in TaroGrid: stop hunting through menus. Press **⌘K** and type three letters of what you want. It's almost always faster.

🛍️ **Example.** Meem wants to know what Pathao owes her. She presses **⌘K**, types "cou", and Courier Cash is the first result. Enter — the window floats over her board. Esc — she's back on her orders. Total: four seconds.
