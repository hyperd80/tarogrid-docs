Every screen in TaroGrid is built from the same five areas. Learn them once; nothing will ever feel unfamiliar again.

:::demo screen-anatomy · "The five areas" · 15s
Numbered labels drop onto the app: ① sidebar, ② board area, ③ module window, ④ ASK bar, ⑤ help.
:::

:::figure screen-anatomy
:::

## The five areas

:::cards
- **① Sidebar** — your company's map: the Money, People, Work, and Outreach categories, and every board. If it exists, it's reachable from here.
- **② Board area** — where a board's rows and columns show. This is your data, in the open.
- **③ Module Windows** — smart screens that float over the board area: Invoice Center, Courier Cash, the Cockpit. Close one and you're back where you were.
- **④ ASK bar** — press **⌘K** anywhere. Find any window or board, ask a question ("revenue this month"), or do work ("create an order for Anika").
- **⑤ Help** — the `?` opens help for the screen you're on, including this article.
:::

## "I want to… → where do I look?"

:::cards
- **See or edit any list** — orders, products, tasks → look at ② the board.
- **Run one area of the business** — invoices, courier cash, the day's overview → open ③ a module window (from ① or ④).
- **Find anything, fast** — press ④ **⌘K** and type three letters.
- **Add a teammate or change settings** — ① the sidebar's bottom section.
- **Understand the screen you're on** — ⑤ the `?`.
:::

:::tip
The fastest habit in TaroGrid: stop hunting through menus. Press **⌘K** and type three letters of what you want. It's almost always faster.
:::

:::example
Maya wants to know what her courier still owes her. She presses **⌘K**, types "cou", and Courier Cash is the first result. Enter — the window floats over her board. Esc — she's back on her orders. Total: four seconds.
:::
