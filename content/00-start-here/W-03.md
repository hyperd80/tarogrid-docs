**Goal:** by the end of this page, your company exists in TaroGrid, your shop link is live, and you've watched a test order arrive by itself.

You don't need to finish all six steps in one sitting. TaroGrid keeps the checklist pinned until you're done.

## The six steps

1. **Name your company.** This creates your Workspace — the home everything else lives in. Use your real business name: it appears on your shop page and your invoices.
2. **Add a product.** Open the **Product Catalog** and add one real product — photo, price, and stock if you track it. One is enough to start.
3. **Publish your shop link.** Build your Sales Form (TaroGrid starts you with the courier-ready fields: name, phone, address), then publish. You get a short link made for chat.
4. **Take a test order.** Open your own shop link on your phone and order as if you were a customer. Watch the order land on your Orders Board — as a row you never typed.
5. **Connect a courier.** Add your Pathao, Steadfast, or RedX merchant details once. After this, booking a parcel is one click from the order row.
6. **Meet ASK.** Press **⌘K** (or click the search bar) and type *"spent 50 on packaging"*. ASK shows you a confirm card. This bar can find anything, answer questions, and do work for you.

**Result:** a customer can now order from your link, the order books a courier without re-typing, and your money side updates itself. That's a working business loop — on day one.

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ **Don't share your link before the test order (step 4).** Two minutes of testing saves you from learning about a problem from a real customer.
- ⚠️ **Don't skip the courier connection because "I'll book manually."** Manual booking means re-typing the address into the courier portal — that's where wrong-address parcels come from.
- 💡 If a step asks for something you don't have yet (say, courier merchant details), skip it and come back. The checklist waits.
