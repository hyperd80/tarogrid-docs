TaroGrid is your whole business in one place — your shop, your orders, your couriers, your money, your people.

You sell. Customers order. Couriers deliver. Cash comes back. TaroGrid holds that entire story together, so nothing gets lost between apps, chats, and notebooks.

:::demo order-ripple · "One order, everything updates" · 28s
1. A customer opens your shop link on their phone. They fill in name, phone, address, and tap **Order (COD)**.
2. The order lands on your **Orders Board** as a new row — automatically. You typed nothing.
3. The fraud check runs and shows 🟢. Stock on the Rose Three-Piece counts down: 12 → 11.
4. You confirm the order — and the courier is booked for you. A tracking ID appears by itself.
5. The money side updates itself: a draft invoice appears, and your "Outstanding" number ticks up.
6. **Courier Cash** starts tracking that ৳2,450 until the courier actually pays you.
:::

## What TaroGrid replaces

Most sellers run their business across five places at once:

- Orders arrive in **Messenger** and scroll away.
- Stock lives in your **memory**, or a notebook.
- The **courier portal** knows the parcel, but your list doesn't.
- Money sits in **Excel** — if you remember to update it.
- "Did I actually make a profit this month?" — nobody knows.

TaroGrid puts all of it in one app, where each part updates the others.

:::why Why one app instead of Messenger + Excel + a courier app + a notebook?
Because your order, your stock, your courier, and your cash are **one story**. Splitting one story across four apps is where money leaks — the order you forgot, the parcel that came back, the courier payout you never checked. When it's one system, nothing falls between the cracks.
:::

## The three ideas that explain everything

You only need three ideas to understand all of TaroGrid. Here they are in short — the next article teaches each one properly.

### 1. Your Workspace is your company

Everything inside a Workspace belongs to that company — its information, its money records, its people, its work. If you run two separate businesses, you give each its own Workspace, and they never mix.

```diagram
┌────────────── WORKSPACE = YOUR COMPANY ──────────────┐
│                                                      │
│   MONEY            PEOPLE            WORK            │
│   (invoices,       (customers,       (your own       │
│    orders,          suppliers,        boards and     │
│    courier cash)    your team)        projects)      │
│                                                      │
│   Underneath it all: BOARDS — the company's          │
│   databases                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2. Boards are your company's databases

A board is a smart ledger book (খাতা). Every board stores one kind of information as rows. Each row is one thing — one order, one customer, one product. Each column is one fact about that thing — name, phone, price, status.

### 3. Module Windows are intelligent screens — not the database

The Invoice Center, Courier Cash, the Command Center — these are **windows** that work *on top of* boards. The window is the cockpit; the board is the engine room. You can always open the engine room and see the raw rows. Nothing is hidden.
