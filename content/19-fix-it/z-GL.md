## The foundations

:::cards
- **Workspace** — your company: everything inside belongs to it, and two never mix. [C-01](#/C-01)
- **Board** — a smart ledger: rows are things, columns are facts. [C-02](#/C-02)
- **System board** — a board a Module Window maintains; the window writes its own columns, you extend it with yours. [C-04](#/C-04)
- **Module Window** — an intelligent screen on top of boards: the cockpit, never the database. [C-03](#/C-03)
- **Group** — a colored section inside a board: a stage you'd say out loud. [C-06](#/C-06)
- **Item (row)** — one real thing: one order, one customer, one task. [C-06](#/C-06)
- **Cell** — one fact about one thing — with a memory of every past value. [C-06](#/C-06)
- **Field (column)** — the kind of fact a column holds: text, number, status, product… [C-02](#/C-02)
- **View** — glasses for a board: Table, Kanban, Calendar. Changing views never changes data. [B-03](#/B-03)
:::

## Selling

:::cards
- **Catalog** — your shelf: every product once, with photo, price, stock. [S-02](#/S-02)
- **Sales Form / shop link** — the public page customers order from — a door into your Orders Board. [S-05](#/S-05)
- **Order** — one row on the Orders Board, written by the form or by you. [S-08](#/S-08)
- **Variant** — one product's options: size, color — each with honest stock. [S-03](#/S-03)
- **Combo / bundle** — products sold as a set at its own price; every member's stock draws down. [S-04](#/S-04)
- **COD (cash on delivery)** — the customer pays the courier at the door; the cash comes home via Courier Cash. [S-09](#/S-09)
- **Fraud detector** — the risk light on every order: green, yellow, red — a signal, not a verdict. [F-21](#/F-21)
- **Advance payment** — a small upfront amount that filters fake orders and confirms real ones. [F-24](#/F-24)
- **Delivery field** — book and track the courier from the order row. [F-20](#/F-20)
- **Courier Cash** — the window that watches COD money until it's truly yours. [S-19](#/S-19)
- **Reconcile** — match a courier's payout to your orders, line by line. [S-19](#/S-19)
:::

## Money

:::cards
- **Invoice** — the formal "you owe me," numbered and immutable once published. [M-04](#/M-04)
- **Draft** — an unpublished invoice: no number, edit freely. [M-02](#/M-02)
- **Receipt** — proof of payment; mints itself when money is verified. [M-01](#/M-01)
- **Expense** — money out, logged in one sentence. [M-09](#/M-09)
- **Net** — money in minus money out: did you make money? [M-01](#/M-01)
- **Payment gateway** — the service that moves card/wallet money; connected once, used everywhere. [M-08](#/M-08)
:::

## People & access

:::cards
- **Stakeholder** — someone you do business WITH: customer, supplier, lead. [C-05](#/C-05)
- **Team** — the people you do business AS: members and their roles. [T-03](#/T-03)
- **Guest** — an outsider who sees exactly one shared board. [C-07](#/C-07)
- **Access Center** — per-module permissions: who, and how much. [T-04](#/T-04)
- **🔒 Hidden** — the honest answer when a value derives from a module you can't access. [T-04](#/T-04)
:::

## The machinery

:::cards
- **Automation / recipe** — a written-down habit: WHEN this, DO that — exactly, every time. [AU-01](#/AU-01)
- **Trigger · Check · Then/Else** — the three slots of every rule. [AU-02](#/AU-02)
- **ASK (⌘K)** — the bar that finds, answers, and acts — instant, private, predictable. [A-01](#/A-01)
- **Confirm card** — ASK's preview of what's about to happen; nothing acts without it. [A-04](#/A-04)
- **Sync** — your device copy and the cloud copy, kept in step; offline edits queue and flow. [X-05](#/X-05)
- **Trash** — where deletes go instead of oblivion; restorable with history. [B-11](#/B-11)
- **Outreach** — campaigns and follow-up sequences: how you reach the people you know. [C-05](#/C-05)
:::
