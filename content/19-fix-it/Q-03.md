*Your Courier Cash number and the courier's message disagree. Good news: the window was built for exactly this moment — the disagreement is pointing at something.*

## First, the principle

Courier Cash **never silently absorbs** a difference ([S-19](#/S-19)). If the numbers disagree, the gap is sitting somewhere visible — usually in **Needs review**. Read the gap; don't fight it.

## The checks, in order

1. **Open Needs review.** Unmatched payout lines live here — a typo'd tracking ID, a parcel from before you used TaroGrid, a line for someone else's shop entirely. Each resolved line moves money to its right place ([S-19](#/S-19)'s example is exactly this).
2. **Deductions are not mismatches.** Couriers pay out *after* fees — delivery charges, COD fees, return charges. A $59 order paying out $52 isn't missing $7; it's the fee, and it belongs in the payout lines as the courier lists them ([S-21](#/S-21)).
3. **Timing.** "To collect" counts *delivered* orders; the courier's app may quote what's in *this week's* payout batch. A parcel delivered today is yours-but-unbatched — both numbers are true, at different clocks.
4. **Unlogged payouts.** Did last week's payout get logged ([S-21](#/S-21)) — or fetched ([S-20](#/S-20))? An unlogged payout leaves everything it covered still showing "to collect."

## When the courier is actually short

Checks done and a real line is missing? Now you have what most sellers never do: **evidence** — this order, this tracking ID, delivered on this date, absent from these payouts. Send exactly that to the courier's merchant support. Specific claims get paid; vague feelings get apologized to.

:::note
The window's strictness exists for this conversation. "You owe me $59 for tracking #TRK-88412, delivered March 12th" is a different negotiation from "I think the total's off."
:::
