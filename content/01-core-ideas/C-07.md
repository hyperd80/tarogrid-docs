*Five roles, three honest states, one rule that never leaks. Everything else about permissions is detail.*

:::demo role-switch · "Same board, three pairs of eyes" · 18s
The owner sees everything → a member without money access simply has no money entries → a guest sees one shared board, and nothing else exists.
:::

## The five roles

:::cards
- **Owner** — the business owner. Everything, including money settings and deciding who sees what.
- **Admin** — a trusted organizer. Manages boards and people — but does *not* automatically see everything; money areas still need to be granted.
- **Member** — a teammate. Works on what they've been given: enter orders, update statuses, book couriers.
- **Viewer** — look, don't touch. Sees what they're granted, edits nothing.
- **Guest** — an outsider, like a client or partner. Exactly one shared board; the rest of your company doesn't exist for them.
:::

## The three honest states

For any module — Invoices, Campaigns, Stakeholders — a person experiences exactly one of three states:

1. **Full access** — they use it like you do.
2. **View only** — they can look, but the editing buttons simply aren't there. A quiet banner says so.
3. **No access** — it's not there at all. No menu entry, no teaser, no locked-door icon to wonder about.

:::note
The owner can set each money/people module to *Everyone*, *Owners only*, or *Chosen people*, and per-person *No access / View / Manage*. That's the whole system: pick who, pick how much.
:::

## The rule that never leaks

:::why Why do I sometimes see 🔒 Hidden instead of a number?
Because a permission that leaks through a side door isn't a permission. Suppose Sana can see customers but not invoices — and a customer card shows "Total billed." If that showed $0, it would be a lie; if it showed the real total, invoices just leaked. So TaroGrid shows **🔒 Hidden** — the honest answer. Any value *derived from* a module you can't access shows as Hidden, everywhere: boards, dashboards, even ASK's answers.
:::

## Example

:::example
**Maya's team, tuned in two minutes.** Sana confirms orders and books couriers → **Member**, with orders access, no Money. Ben answers messages and sometimes checks stock → **Viewer** on the Catalog. Maya's wholesale supplier wants to see the restock plan → **Guest** on that one board. Nobody sees the net profit but Maya. Nobody feels spied on, either — what's closed is simply not there.
:::

## What to avoid

- ⚠️ Don't share the owner account "for convenience." Give the person a role instead — history and undo work best when every change has a real name on it.
- Don't grant everything to everyone on day one. Start narrow; widen when someone actually hits a wall. Widening is one click; un-leaking knowledge isn't.

## Best practices

- ✔ Give people the smallest access that lets them do their whole job.
- ✔ Use Guest for outsiders — never a member account with "please don't look around."
- ✔ When someone reports "I can't see X" — that's usually permissions working. Check their role before suspecting a bug.
