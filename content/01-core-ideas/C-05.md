## Overview

Every Workspace has the same three categories:

- **Money** — everything about money in and money out.
- **People** — every human and company you deal with.
- **Work** — your own boards, your own way of running things.

Money and People come **pre-built**, because every business needs them the same way. Work is different — it's **your blank canvas**.

## Money — in and out, nothing vague

Money holds: invoices, receipts, payments and pay links, expenses, purchases, advance payments, courier cash (your COD money), and the one number that matters — **net: did I make money?**

:::why Why is Money pre-built and locked down?
Because mistakes here cost real taka. Invoice numbers must never repeat. A published invoice must never quietly change. A courier payout must match your rows exactly. TaroGrid builds these rules in so you can't lose money to a slip — your own experiments belong in Work, where nothing expensive can break.
:::

## People — with you, and as you

People holds every human and company around your business — and it makes one distinction early, because it matters:

- **Stakeholders** = people you do business **WITH**: customers, suppliers, vendors, leads.
- **Team** = people you do business **AS**: your members, their roles, their invites.

Anika (a customer) is a stakeholder. Shanta (who packs your parcels) is team. Different worlds, different screens, different rules.

## Work — your own operating system

Work is your workspace-within-the-workspace. Your own boards, in any shape. Your own workflows. Your own projects, your own CRM, your own operations.

It runs on the **same engine** that powers Money and People — the same boards, fields, views, and automations — but with *your* rules. Nothing in Work is locked, because everything in Work is yours.

## Example

🛍️ **Where Meem's things live.** Anika's unpaid invoice → **Money**. Anika herself → **People** (a stakeholder). Shanta → **People** (team). Meem's Eid photo-shoot plan, her content calendar, her packing checklist → **Work**, on boards she shaped herself.

## Relationship with other features

- The sidebar ([A tour of the screen](#/W-04)) is organized by exactly these three categories.
- Each category's screens are Module Windows over boards — [Idea 3](#/C-03), everywhere.
- **Permissions** ([C-07](#/C-07)) can open or close whole categories per person — Shanta can work orders without ever seeing Money.

## What to avoid

- ⚠️ Don't rebuild invoices or expenses by hand in Work "to do it my way." You'd be trading away the money safety rules — numbering, immutability, reconciliation — that exist to protect you. Extend the built-ins instead (add your own columns; see [the lock](#/C-04)).
- Don't put teammates in Stakeholders or customers in Team. WITH vs AS — the distinction pays off everywhere later.

## Best practices

- ✔ New thing to track? Ask: is it money, a person, or my own process? That's its home.
- ✔ Keep experiments in Work. When a Work process starts touching money, connect it to the Money built-ins rather than copying them.
