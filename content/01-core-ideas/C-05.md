*Your Workspace organizes itself into four worlds. Once you know what belongs where, you'll never hunt for anything again.*

:::demo four-zones · "The workspace splits into four" · 20s
Four zones appear: Money · People · Work · Outreach → an invoice flies into Money, a customer into People, a task board into Work, a campaign into Outreach.
:::

## Overview

Every Workspace has the same four categories:

:::cards
- **Money** — everything about money in and money out: invoices, receipts, payments, expenses, courier cash, and the net.
- **People** — every human and company you deal with: customers and suppliers, plus your own team.
- **Work** — your own boards, your own workflows — your blank canvas.
- **Outreach** — the messages your business sends to win and keep customers: campaigns, follow-up sequences, and who replied.
:::

Money, People, and Outreach come **pre-built**, because every business needs them the same way. Work is different — it's yours to shape.

An easy way to hold all four: **Money** is what you earn, **People** is who you know, **Outreach** is how you reach them, and **Work** is everything you run your own way.

## Money — in and out, nothing vague

Money holds: invoices, receipts, payments and pay links, expenses, purchases, advance payments, courier cash (your cash-on-delivery money), and the one number that matters — **net: did I make money?**

:::why Why is Money pre-built instead of yours to shape?
Because mistakes here cost real money. Invoice numbers must never repeat. A published invoice must never quietly change. A courier payout must match your rows exactly. TaroGrid builds these rules in so you can't lose money to a slip — your own experiments belong in Work, where nothing expensive can break.
:::

## People — with you, and as you

People holds every human and company around your business — and it makes one distinction early, because it matters:

- **Stakeholders** = people you do business **WITH**: customers, suppliers, vendors, leads.
- **Team** = people you do business **AS**: your members, their roles, their invites.

Anika (a customer) is a stakeholder. Sana (who packs your parcels) is team. Different worlds, different screens, different rules.

## Work — your own operating system

Work is your workspace-within-the-workspace. Your own boards, in any shape. Your own workflows. Your own projects, your own CRM, your own operations.

It runs on the **same engine** that powers the other categories — the same boards, fields, views, and automations — but with *your* rules. Nothing in Work is pre-built for you, because everything in Work is yours to design.

## Outreach — how you reach the people you know

Outreach holds your campaigns: email sequences that introduce, follow up, and win back — and the living record of every contact: sent, opened, clicked, replied.

It works hand in hand with People: the *contacts* live in People; Outreach is what you *send* them. And like Money, it rides a system board the module writes (the Email Outreach board — see [who writes what](#/C-04)) with protections built in: daily sending limits, unsubscribes honored forever, sending inside business hours.

:::note
Those guardrails aren't restrictions — they're deposits into your sender reputation. Mail that respects people keeps landing in inboxes.
:::

## Example

:::example
**Where Maya's things live.** Anika's unpaid invoice → **Money**. Anika herself → **People** (a stakeholder). Sana → **People** (team). The three-email campaign nudging quiet customers before the holidays → **Outreach**. Maya's photo-shoot plan, her content calendar, her packing checklist → **Work**, on boards she shaped herself.
:::

## Relationship with other features

- The sidebar ([A tour of the screen](#/W-04)) is organized by exactly these four categories.
- Each category's screens are Module Windows over boards — [Idea 3](#/C-03), everywhere.
- **Permissions** ([C-07](#/C-07)) can open or close whole categories per person — Sana can work orders without ever seeing Money or Outreach.

## What to avoid

- ⚠️ Don't rebuild invoices or expenses by hand in Work "to do it my way." You'd be trading away the money safety rules — numbering, immutability, reconciliation — that exist to protect you. Extend the built-ins instead (add your own columns; see [who writes what](#/C-04)).
- ⚠️ Don't feed Outreach a cold purchased list. It's built for people who expect to hear from you — the guardrails will resist mass-blasting on purpose, and that's them working.
- Don't put teammates in Stakeholders or customers in Team. WITH vs AS — the distinction pays off everywhere later.

## Best practices

- ✔ New thing to track? Ask: is it money, a person, a message you send, or my own process? That's its home.
- ✔ Keep experiments in Work. When a Work process starts touching money, connect it to the Money built-ins rather than copying them.
