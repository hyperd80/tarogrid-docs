*The most common "bug report" in TaroGrid isn't a bug. It's this article.*

:::demo module-writes-back · "Who writes what, and where your edit goes" · 21s
Built-in boards open like any other → you fix a status by hand and it saves → but the module owns that column → its next refresh takes it back → the safe way: your own column, never touched.
:::

## Overview

Every built-in Module Window keeps its data on a real board underneath — one window, one board:

:::cards
- ✍️ **Invoice Center** — writes the *Invoices* board
- ✍️ **Receipts window** — writes the *Receipts* board
- ✍️ **Expenses window** — writes the *Expenses* board
- ✍️ **Stakeholders window** — writes the *Stakeholders* board
- ✍️ **Campaigns / Outreach** — writes the *Email Outreach* board
- ✍️ **Model → Deals** — writes the *Deals* board
- ✍️ **Product Catalog** — writes the *Inventory* board
:::

Those seven are the ones a module **rebuilds**, and this article is mostly about them. The **Orders** board is a built-in too, but it's *your* working board: the shop writes the columns it manages (items, totals, payment) and leaves the rest to you — and unlike the others, you can rename it.

## Why they exist

So a window always has a real database under it. You get the friendly cockpit *and* full access to the raw rows — nothing hidden, ever. That's the promise from [Module Windows](#/C-03), kept honestly.

## What changed: the padlock is gone

These boards used to arrive **locked** — read-only, nothing editable. They don't any more. Every built-in board now opens like any other board: you can type in it, add rows, add columns. Boards you already had unlocked themselves.

That's more freedom, and it comes with one thing you now have to know yourself, because the app no longer stops you:

:::warning The app will let you type into a column it's going to overwrite
Nothing bounces any more. Your edit saves, looks correct, and sits there — until the module refreshes that column and writes its own value back over yours. No warning, no error. **This is the one trade the unlock made.**
:::

## Who writes what

In plain terms: **two writers, one cell, guaranteed tears.** Every column on these boards has exactly one writer — either a module, or you. Editing a column the module writes is the only way to lose work here.

The good news: **the board tells you which columns came built in.** On a built-in board:

- A **built-in column** has a *lighter, greyer* heading, no delete button, and a tooltip reading **"Built-in column."** Try to rename it and TaroGrid says *"This is a built-in column — its name can't be changed."*
- **A column you added** has a **bold** heading, like every column on an ordinary board — and it is always, entirely yours.

Most built-in columns are the module's to write. But a few are deliberately seeded **for you to fill**, and a refresh never touches them:

:::tip Built-in columns that are yours to fill
On **Stakeholders**: **Type · Stage · Owner · Notes** — the window can't know these, so they're left for you.
On **Email Outreach**: **Outcome** — the module tracks sent/opened/clicked; how it *ended* is your call.
:::

So the rule, in one line: **a column you added is always safe. A built-in column is safe only if it's one of the few above — otherwise it belongs to the module.**

## The safe pattern: add your own column

To put your own information on a built-in board, **add your own column** — notes, tags, a custom "Outcome," anything. No unlocking needed; it just works.

Your own columns are never in a module's plan, so a refresh skips them entirely. They survive every refresh, every sync, forever.

One sentence to remember: **extend the board, never fight the module.**

## What's still permanent

The lock is gone, but these boards are still *structural* — a module finds its board and its columns **by name**, so a rename is silent data loss. TaroGrid protects that for you:

- ⚠️ A built-in board **can't be deleted** — *"This is a built-in board — it can't be deleted."* Hide it instead if it's in your way.
- ⚠️ A built-in board **can't be renamed** (Orders is the one exception).
- ⚠️ A module's columns **can't be renamed or deleted**.

Your own rows and columns on those boards stay fully yours to rename, delete, and rearrange.

## Want the old protection back?

You can lock any board yourself: **Board settings → Lock board**, or the board's ⋯ menu → **Lock board**. That freezes every row and column — read-only for everyone until it's unlocked. If a teammate keeps hand-editing invoice statuses, this is the fix.

## Example

:::example
**Maya's two columns.** Maya wants to track how each outreach contact ended: *Working / Purchased / Declined*. The Email Outreach board already has an **Outcome** column waiting — grey heading, but seeded for exactly this. She fills it in, and no campaign refresh ever overwrites it.

Then she wants something the board doesn't have: which packaging photo she sent. So she **adds her own column**, "Photo sent" — bold heading, entirely hers.

What she carefully does *not* do: retype **Opened** to correct it. That one the module writes, and it would be back by morning.
:::

## Where this bites you

- Correcting a status, amount or date on the Invoices, Receipts, Expenses, Deals, Stakeholders, Email Outreach or Inventory boards — the window writes those, and wins.
- Naming your own new column the same as a module's column (say, a second "Status"). The module writes to **whatever column carries that name** — so it will adopt yours and overwrite it. Pick a different name.
- Typing rows straight onto a mirror board. They won't hurt anything, but the module won't know about them, so they stay dead rows.
- Meeting it in the wild: *["I edited a cell and it changed back"](#/Q-07)* — this same lesson, from the other end.

## What to avoid

- ⚠️ Don't "fix" a wrong invoice amount by editing the Invoices board. Fix it in the **Invoice Center** — the writer. The board always follows the writer.
- ⚠️ Don't repurpose a module's column to store your own data. It doesn't know your intention — it knows its column, and it will take it back.

## Best practices

- ✔ Want extra facts on a built-in board? Add *your own* column. Done — no unlock, no risk.
- ✔ Unsure whose column it is? Check the heading. Bold = you added it, always safe. Grey = built-in, so it's the module's unless it's one of the fill-in-yourself columns above.
- ✔ Change data through the window it belongs to; keep hand edits for your own columns.
- ✔ Sharing a board with people who don't know this rule? **Lock board** is one switch away.
