*The most common "bug report" in TaroGrid isn't a bug. It's this article.*

:::demo locked-cell · "The lock, and the right way around it" · 22s

2. Caption: "The Invoice Center writes this column. Hand edits would be overwritten."
3. The owner unlocks the board and adds a **new** column: "Notes".

## Overview

Every built-in Module Window keeps its data on a **hidden, locked system board**:

| Window (the cockpit) | System board underneath |
|---|---|
| Invoice Center | Invoice Board *(locked)* |
| Receipts window | Receipt Board *(locked)* |
| Expenses window | Expenses Board *(locked)* |
| Stakeholders window | Stakeholders Board *(locked)* |
| Campaigns / Outreach | Outreach Board *(locked)* |
| Sales Form / Shop | Orders Board *(the columns it manages are locked)* |

## Why they exist

So a window always has a real database under it. You get the friendly cockpit *and* full access to the raw rows — nothing hidden, ever. That's the promise from [Module Windows](#/C-03), kept honestly.

## Why they're locked

:::why Why can't I just edit the cell?
Because the window is the writer. If you hand-edit a cell the window manages, the window will faithfully rewrite it on the next refresh — and your edit silently disappears. That's heartbreak, not help. The lock exists to prevent exactly that heartbreak: it stops the edit *before* you waste effort on it.
:::

In plain terms: **two writers, one cell, guaranteed tears.** The lock makes sure each module-managed column has exactly one writer — the module.

## Why you may unlock

To make the board *yours* — on top of what the module manages:

- Add **your own columns**: notes, tags, a custom "Outcome," anything.
- Edit columns marked as **user-owned**.

TaroGrid marks which columns are module-managed and which are yours. Your columns survive every refresh, every sync, forever.

## The consequences, said plainly

- ⚠️ Editing a **module-managed** column after unlocking = the module wins on the next sync. Your edit will vanish. This is by design, and now you know why.
- ⚠️ **Deleting a system board** = the window loses its database. It's recoverable from Trash, but the window sits empty until you restore it.
- ⚠️ **Never rename or repurpose a module-managed column** to store your own data. The module doesn't know your intention — it knows its column, and it will take it back.

One sentence to remember: **unlock to extend, never to fight the module.**

## Example

🛍️ **Meem's Outcome column.** Meem wants to track how each outreach contact ended: *Working / Purchased / Declined*. The Outreach Board is locked — the Campaigns window manages it. So she unlocks it and adds her own column, "Outcome." Every campaign refresh updates the module's columns — sent, opened, clicked — and never touches hers. Two years of campaigns later, her Outcome column is still exactly as she left it.

## Where you'll meet the lock

- Trying to edit a cell on the Invoice, Receipt, Expenses, Stakeholders, or Outreach boards.
- On the Orders Board — where the columns the shop manages (products, totals, fraud) are locked, and yours aren't.
- In the future troubleshooting article *"I edited a cell and it changed back"* — which is this lesson, met in the wild.

## What to avoid

- ⚠️ Don't "fix" a wrong invoice amount by editing the Invoice Board. Fix it in the **Invoice Center** — the writer. The board will follow, because it always follows the writer.
- Don't delete a system board to "clean up." Empty is not clean — it's a window with no engine.

## Best practices

- ✔ Want extra facts on a system board? Unlock, add *your own* column, done.
- ✔ Unsure whose column it is? TaroGrid marks module-managed columns — check before you type.
- ✔ Change data through the window it belongs to; keep hand edits for your own columns and your own boards.
