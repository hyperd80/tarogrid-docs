## Read the map left to right

- Your **Catalog** is the single truth for products, prices, and stock.
- Your **shop link** is a door: customers walk through it, and their order lands on the **Orders Board** as a row — with the fraud light already on and stock already counted down.
- From the row, the **courier** is booked without re-typing. Delivery status updates itself.
- Delivered cash-on-delivery money appears in **Courier Cash** — tracked per courier until the payout actually reaches you.
- The **Money** side follows along: invoices, receipts, expenses, and finally the net.
- **People** quietly keeps one row per real human through all of it.
- **Outreach** reads People and reaches out — campaigns and follow-ups — then reports back who opened and replied.
- **Work** is yours — connected to the rest whenever you want it to be.

## Why the arrows matter more than the boxes

:::why Why is "connected" the whole point?
Every arrow on this map is a chore you no longer do — and an error you can no longer make. No copying an address into a courier portal. No updating stock in a second app. No wondering which spreadsheet has the real total. When one thing changes, everything downstream already knows. That's not a feature of TaroGrid; it *is* TaroGrid.
:::

## Example

:::example
**One evening at Maya's Closet.** 9:12 pm: Anika orders through the shop link. By 9:13, without anyone touching anything: the row exists, the fraud light is green, stock reads 11, and the money side knows an order worth $59 is in play. Next morning Sana taps **Confirm** — the courier is booked. Three days later, "Delivered" arrives on its own, and Courier Cash starts waiting for the payout alongside Maya. Maya did two things in this story: confirmed once, and slept well.
:::
