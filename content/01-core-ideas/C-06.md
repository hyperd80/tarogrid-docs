*Every board in TaroGrid — yours or the system's — is built from the same four layers. Learn them here, use them everywhere.*

:::demo board-zoom · "Board → group → row → cell" · 16s
1. The camera starts on a whole board.
2. It zooms to one colored **group** band ("New").
3. Then to one **row** — Anika's order.
4. Then to one **cell** — Total: ৳2,450 — and small history dots appear beside it: who set it, and when.
:::

## The four layers

```diagram
BOARD                 the whole ledger ("Orders")
  ▸ GROUPS            colored sections ("New" / "Shipped" / "Done")
      ▸ ITEMS         rows — one per real thing (one order)
          ▸ CELLS     one fact each (this order's Phone, this order's Total)
```

**Board** — one kind of information. Orders. Products. Leads. One board per kind.

**Groups** — colored sections *inside* a board. Use them for stages or sections you'd say out loud: "New / Shipped / Done." Groups keep a big board readable at a glance.

**Items** — the rows. One row is one real thing in your business. Anika's order of March 3rd is one row, forever.

**Cells** — where a row meets a column. One cell holds exactly one fact: *this* order's phone number, *this* order's total.

## The quiet memory

Every cell remembers its history: what it said before, who changed it, and when.

This is why TaroGrid can offer real undo, and why "who changed this price?" always has an answer. Nothing you do to a cell is ever a mystery later.

💬 **Note** — history isn't a special feature you turn on. It's simply how boards work, everywhere, including system boards.

## Why this shape?

:::why Why rows-and-columns instead of free-form notes?
Because one-fact-per-cell is what makes intelligence possible. TaroGrid can sum a Total column, filter by a City column, or watch a Status column for automation — only because each fact has its own labeled place. A paragraph that says "Anika, Mirpur, paid ৳2,450" is readable by you; a row with Customer, City, and Total cells is readable by you *and* by every smart feature in the app.
:::

## Example

🛍️ **One order, layer by layer.** Board: *Orders*. Group: *New*. Item: Anika's order, arrived 9:12 pm from the shop link. Cells: Customer *Anika* · Phone *017…* · Products *Rose Three-Piece ×1* · Total *৳2,450* · Status *Pending*. When Shanta confirms it tomorrow, the Status cell will change — and remember that Shanta changed it, at 10:03 am.

## What to avoid

- Don't use groups for things that belong in a column. *"New / Shipped / Done"* — good groups. *"Anika's orders / Tanvir's orders"* — that's a Customer column doing a group's job badly.
- Don't create a new board when a new *group* would do. New kind of information → new board. New stage of the same information → new group.

## Best practices

- ✔ Name groups as stages you'd say out loud to a teammate.
- ✔ One row = one real thing. If you're tempted to put two orders in one row, it's two rows.
- ✔ Trust the history. Deleted something? Changed your mind? The cell remembers; undo is always safe.
