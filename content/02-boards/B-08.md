**Goal:** ask a board questions — "which Pending orders are in Riverside?" — and get the answer as a live, shrinking list.

:::demo filter-stack · "Questions as chips" · 14s
Filters stack like sentences: Status is Pending… and City is Riverside → the count updates live → clear the chips, everything returns.
:::

## The four tools

:::cards
- **Filter** — show only rows where… *Status is Pending*, *Total over $50*. Filters **stack**: each chip narrows further, and the row count updates live.
- **Sort** — order rows by a column: newest first, biggest total first.
- **Group** — re-band the board by any field's values: by courier, by assignee, by status ([the dispatch wall trick, S-18](#/S-18)).
- **Search** — type a fragment, see matching rows. The fastest "where's Anika's order?"
:::

## The safety fact

Filters **hide, never delete**. A filtered board isn't a smaller board — it's the same board wearing a question. Clear the chips and all rows return, untouched.

:::why Why chips instead of a filter menu buried somewhere?
Because you should always *see what question the board is answering*. Chips sit in daylight: "Status: Pending ✕ · City: Riverside ✕". Half of all "my rows disappeared!" moments are a forgotten filter — visible chips make the question, and the fix, one glance ([Q-02](#/Q-02) starts here).
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Check the chips before panicking about missing rows — the row didn't leave; your question excluded it.
- 💡 A filter you rebuild daily deserves to be saved with its view — same board, ready-made glasses.

## Example

:::example
"Which unpaid orders over $50 are older than a week?" — three chips: *Status is not Paid · Total > 50 · Created before last Monday*. Fourteen rows. Maya works the list top to bottom, clears the chips, and the board is whole again.
:::
