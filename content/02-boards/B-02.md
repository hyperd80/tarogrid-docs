*A row is one line of facts. The Item panel is that row, unfolded.*

:::demo item-panel · "One thing, unfolded" · 12s
Click a row — the panel slides in → everything about one thing: fields, files, comments, and who changed what.
:::

## What's in the panel

Click any row and the **Item panel** slides in:

:::cards
- **Every field** — including columns hidden in your current view. The row's full truth, editable in place.
- **Files** — photos, documents, attachments that belong to this one thing.
- **Comments** — a conversation attached to the item, @mention a teammate and they're notified.
- **Activity** — the quiet history: who changed what, when ([the cell memory, C-06](#/C-06)).
:::

## When to open it

The board is for scanning many things; the panel is for *being with one thing*. An order that needs a decision, a lead you're about to call, a task with a story — open it, and everything you need is one screen instead of six chats.

:::tip
Comments beat chat apps for item-talk: *"customer asked to deliver after 6 pm"* written on the order stays with the order — findable next week, visible to whoever picks up the row. The same message in chat is gone by Thursday.
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ Don't keep item facts in comments ("actually the qty is 3") — facts belong in fields, where sorting and automations can see them. Comments are for the conversation *around* the facts.

## Example

:::example
Sana opens Anika's order before packing: the address field, the *"gift — no price tag please"* comment from Maya, and the photo Anika sent of the color she meant. One panel, zero scrolling through chats. The parcel goes out right the first time.
:::
