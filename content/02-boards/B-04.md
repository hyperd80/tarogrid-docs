**Goal:** make the Table view feel like a well-worn tool — fast at ten rows, still fast at ten thousand.

:::demo table-mastery · "Built for volume" · 10s
The header pins while you scroll thousands of rows → drag columns into your order, resize to fit your eyes.
:::

## The habits that compound

- **The header stays.** Scroll as deep as you like — column labels stay pinned overhead, so row 4,000 is as readable as row 4.
- **Drag columns into *your* order.** The facts you check constantly belong on the left. Every board remembers its arrangement.
- **Resize for your eyes** — drag the column edge; wide for addresses, narrow for checkboxes.
- **Keyboard first:** arrows move between cells, Enter opens a cell for editing, Esc backs out, and typing into a selected cell just starts editing. Editing a column top to bottom with arrows-and-Enter is dramatically faster than click-click-click.

:::tip
TaroGrid keeps a 10,000-row board responsive — so never split "Orders 2024" from "Orders" for speed. One board, one truth; the Table can take it ([the two-boards trap, C-02](#/C-02)).
:::

## Pitfalls

- ⚠️ If you're endlessly widening a "Notes" column to read paragraphs, that's the [Item panel's](#/B-02) job — the Table is for facts at a glance.
- 💡 A column you never look at? Hide it in this view — hiding is per-view glasses, the data stays whole ([B-03](#/B-03)).

## Example

:::example
End of month, Maya checks forty orders' totals against payouts: Total column dragged next to Customer, sorted descending, arrow-down through the list. Four minutes, no mouse after the first click, headers overhead the whole way down.
:::
